A Message from the Chair

Every Democrat we help elect stays on our radar after they take the oath. We track votes, attend hearings, and stay in contact with the people we put in office to make sure they're delivering for Troy — in City Hall, Albany, and Washington. Taxpayer money should be spent carefully. Services should work. We hold our elected officials to that standard.

We look for candidates who already know this city: small business owners, first responders, working parents who've dealt with the same property taxes and infrastructure problems as their neighbors. People who don't need a briefing on what Troy's streets look like. We get them on the ballot, run their campaigns, and turn out the vote.

The committee's job doesn't end on Election Day. We connect Troy residents with the officials and services they're owed, and we push back when government isn't responsive. If you want to get involved — or just want to know what's happening in your city — come to a meeting or reach out directly. We'd be glad to hear from you.

Laura Amos

Chair, City of Troy Democratic Committee
