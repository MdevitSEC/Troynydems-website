A Message from the Chair

The Democrats we elect don't stop needing support the moment they take the oath. Our committee is here to assist every Democrat serving Troy in City Hall, Albany, or Washington, DC. We keep track of what's happening here, connect people with the officials they represent, and make sure that those in office are helping the families of this city. Good government takes ongoing work, and we're committed to doing that work alongside our elected officials.

Troy needs Democrats on the ballot in every district, at every level, every cycle. We find candidates who know these streets, understand the pressures Troy residents face, and are ready to do the job. We help them run. From the first petition signature to GOTV on Election Day, the Troy Democratic Committee (TDC) is in it with them, knocking doors, making calls, and turning out the vote in every neighborhood in this city.

More than anything, this committee exists for the people of Troy. City government should work for the families in Lansingburgh, the renters in South Troy, the small business owners in the Downtown Business Improvement District, and every neighborhood in between. We want to hear from you, organize with you, and fight alongside you. Come to a meeting, join us at one of our social hours, or reach out directly. Troy is worth the effort, and we want you to help us with it.

Laura Amos

Chair, City of Troy Democratic Committee
