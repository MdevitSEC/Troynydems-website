Synopsis

Good Cause Eviction (Local Law 1) passed 7-0 on March 5, making Troy the 18th New York State municipality to opt in under Article 6-A of the Real Property Law. The mayor had requested a pause for an independent economic study; the council voted to proceed. Mayor Mantello later vetoed the law; the council overrode that veto 7-0 on April 9. Remote participation by videoconferencing (Local Law 2) also passed 7-0.

Council President Steele opened the session with the annual Legislative Address, outlining the new council's priorities: transparency, housing justice, climate action, public safety reform, infrastructure investment, and fiscal oversight. Assemblyman John McDonald, Governor Hochul's Regional Representative Dan Ren, Mayor Mantello, and a contingent from the fire service attended. Steele committed to ending same-day or last-minute introduction of major borrowing requests: "The days of that are over."

The post-meeting forum was the first substantive council session on Flock Safety cameras. Twelve speakers testified unanimously for contract termination, raising concerns about privacy, Fourth Amendment violations, ICE data sharing, and the vendor's data practices. The forum set the stage for the overflow hearing at the March 19 Committee Night.

Legislative Address Highlights

Transparency & Process

Committee process reinstituted; remote participation being enacted tonight; quarterly financial reports to be delivered per law. "Multi-million dollar borrowing requests advanced with less than 24 hours notice: the days of that are over."

Housing

Good Cause Eviction receiving final approval tonight; landlord registry to be pursued; rental inspection/occupancy certification program to be advanced.

Climate

Climate Smart Community Task Force and coordinator to be established (Res. 36 and 37, introduced tonight for Finance Committee on March 19).

Infrastructure

Lead service line replacement pace: "woefully inadequate." The 2025 goal was 1,000; actual replacements were ~178. "At its current replacement rate, we would be lucky if we were finished within 20 years." Riverwalk Park North extension has $4M in new state funding.

City Hall

Project advanced through prior council on "accelerated timeline with limited transparency." A lawsuit has been filed. Called for full taxpayer transparency as the process continues.

Public Safety

Social media policy for city communications to be advanced; specific criticism of recent city hall social media posts straying from professional standards.

Good Cause Eviction: Council Comments

McKee: Protects residents from arbitrary eviction; still allows evictions for non-payment, lease violations, property damage; allows rents to exceed the cap to recover documented cost increases.

Campbell-Cohen: Thanked district volunteers for years of canvassing work. Noted the 30-year new-construction exemption.

Spain-McLaren: Noted the irony of the mayor's request for a housing study: at their one meeting during the last term, Spain-McLaren proposed a housing study and the mayor said "absolutely not." "Now all of a sudden when it looks like we are going to be able to pass good cause eviction, we now need to do a housing study."
