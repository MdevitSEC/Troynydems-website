STATEMENT FROM TROY COUNCIL PRESIDENT SUE STEELE ON 2026 STATE OF THE STATE

Troy, New York (Jan. 13, 2026) – City Council President Sue Steele issued the following statement:

“Governor Hochul’s State of the State address outlined her plans to help reduce costs for New Yorkers battered by Trump’s tariffs, protect and support our children at school and at home, and investments in critical infrastructure and much-needed affordable housing. Families in communities like Troy need leaders that care about the people they represent, and Governor Hochul has committed to fighting for a safer, stronger, more resilient future for all of us.”
