Make Your Mark on Troy

Troy Democrats hold the majority on the City Council, and we are committed to building a strong bench of candidates who reflect our community and share our values. Running for office as a Democrat in Troy means joining a coalition that is actively working to deliver results for residents — on housing, public safety, infrastructure, and accountable government.

We are always looking for thoughtful, community-minded people who are ready to step up. Whether you have considered running for a long time or the idea is just taking shape, we encourage you to reach out. Every strong Democratic candidate makes Troy stronger.

What We Offer Candidates

Campaign Guidance

We will walk you through the process from petition gathering to Election Day, so you are never navigating it alone.

Volunteer Network

Tap into our network of committed Troy Democrats ready to canvass, phone bank, and organize on your behalf.

Community Connections

We will connect you to neighborhood leaders, stakeholders, and community organizations across Troy's districts.

Fundraising Support

Guidance on building your donor base, organizing fundraisers, and running a financially competitive campaign.

Messaging & Outreach

Support crafting your message and reaching voters through door-to-door canvassing, phone banking, and events.

A Democratic Team

Join a coalition of elected officials, committee members, and activists who are invested in Troy's Democratic future.
