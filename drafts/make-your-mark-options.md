## With Specifics

### Option 1 — The Record

Troy's Democratic council majority passed Good Cause Eviction, overrode the mayor's veto 7-0, and made Troy the 20th New York municipality to adopt the law. It appointed the council's first independent legal counsel, enacted a surveillance ordinance governing the city's use of automated license plate readers, and is advancing legislation to reform the city's emergency declaration powers.

Running for office as a Democrat means joining that record and helping build on it. We are looking for candidates who are grounded in Troy and ready to do the work. Reach out and we will walk you through the path from the first step.

Troy tenants now have Good Cause Eviction protections because this council passed the law, overrode a mayoral veto 7-0, and made Troy the 20th New York municipality to adopt it. Running as a Democrat in Troy means serving the people who show up. We are looking for candidates who know their neighborhoods and are committed to their neighbors. Reach out and we will walk you through what the path looks like.
