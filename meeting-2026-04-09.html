April 9, 2026 — Troy City Council

Synopsis

The council voted 7-0 to override Mayor Mantello's veto of Good Cause Eviction on April 9, making Troy the 20th municipality in New York State to opt into Article 6-A of the Real Property Law. The Finance Committee had convened at 6:30 PM with the full council and approximately 20 members of the public before the Regular Meeting opened at 7:14 PM.

The Finance Committee approved a five-year license agreement with BabblingBrookNY Corporation for the Frear Park restaurant concession. Operator Brad had run the tent concession through 2025. Year 1 rent is $1,500/month, escalating to $5,500/month in Year 2 when the permanent building opens. Council members raised questions about a blank in the special event fee provision (a 2% revenue share is under discussion), the permanent building design process, and the administration's public announcement of the deal before council approval.

In the post-meeting public forum, CSEA union representatives described a months-long contract dispute in which the administration failed to respond to a draft integrated contract delivered in November 2025 and proposed reading the contract line-by-line as its first substantive engagement, a suggestion every labor relations specialist contacted described as unprecedented. CSEA had subsequently filed improper practice charges with PERB.
