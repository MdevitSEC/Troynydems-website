Friends, neighbors, and fellow Trojans,

I hope you will join us at City Hall (433 River Street, 5th floor) on Thursday night for the annual Legislative Address, delivered during the regular council meeting on March 5, 2026, beginning at 7 p.m.

The address is an opportunity for the council to provide the public an update on what’s happening in our city and outline our priorities for the coming year.

As always, don’t forget to subscribe for email updates on upcoming meetings and legislation under consideration by the council: https://troyny.gov/315/City-Council

Onward,

Sue Steele
Council President

PS: if you’re unable to attend in person, the event will stream live on the council’s official YouTube channel.
